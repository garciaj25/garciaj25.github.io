How these are derived. The jockey pump holds the top of the band and makes up small leakage. If pressure keeps falling past the jockey's start point, the fire pump starts — that's a real demand. The last card is the safety check: churn plus the highest suction is where you risk exceeding component ratings.

Fire pump stopping. The 2019 edition generally expects the fire pump to be stopped manually. The stop pressure shown is the setpoint the annex example uses; only use automatic stop where your AHJ accepts it, and note a minimum-run timer keeps the pump running at churn past the stop pressure.

Set against calibrated gauges. Where a pressure switch's differential won't allow the exact math, set as close as the equipment permits and confirm with test gauges — uncalibrated gauges can read 15–30% off.
